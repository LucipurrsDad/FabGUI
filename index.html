How to use FabGUI

  • Click an asset (picture or title) to open the download prompt.
  • Right-click an asset to copy its listing UID to the clipboard.
  • Downloads are stored in ~/Downloads/content
  • Authentication handles signing in/out with Epic / Fab through FabCLI itself.
  • This GUI lists your claimed (owned) Fab assets, sorted alphabetically. 5.8 is listed first for convenience.
