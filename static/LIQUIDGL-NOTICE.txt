# liquidGL integration notice

FabGUI's WebGL 2 shader adapts the rounded-rectangle bevel profile,
refraction sampling and dispersion of NaughtyDuk's liquidGL v3.0.0
(https://github.com/naughtyduk/liquidGL), used under the MIT License.
See static/liquidGL/LICENSE.

FabGUI applies the adapted optics to its own fixed wallpaper texture using
Snell's law and viewport-relative CSS pixel geometry.
